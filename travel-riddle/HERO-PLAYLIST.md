# Hero playlist — 1.7.0

Playback order: supplied clips 1–14, Amsterdam, then trekking.

The old ninth clip (Barcelona/Park Güell) and new clip 15 (Barcelona/Sagrada Família) are excluded. Original files are retained for recovery but are not referenced by the homepage or included in the install archive.

Web editions use `hero-N-web.mp4`: 1280×720 H.264, fast-start MP4. Amsterdam is asset 16; trekking is asset 17. Cappadocia (3) uses the opening four seconds to avoid the later direction reversal. The additional CSS zoom has been removed. Clips advance on completion rather than looping within an eight-second timer.

Venice (9) retains its original AAC audio. Autoplay starts muted for browser compatibility; its Sound on button enables the audio after a visitor clicks. Other clips remain silent. Pause/Play and Next controls are provided without slide-count bars.

The homepage header is transparent over the video; logo artwork and slogan are unchanged. Inner-page headers retain their existing styling.
